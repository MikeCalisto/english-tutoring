Present Simple Spelling

he / she / it

SPELLING

play, work → plays, worksgeneral rule: add -s
watch, miss → watches, missesafter -s, -ch, -sh, -z: add -es
study, try → studies, triesafter consonant + -y: delete -y and add -ies
do, go → does, goesafter -o: add -es
have → hasirregular form

ADVERBS OF FREQUENCY

Adverbs of frequency (often, always, usually, sometimes, rarely / seldom, never) are placed before main verbs but after auxiliary and modal verbs (be, have, can, will, must, shall).

He often goes to the theatre.
He is never late.
Tonia doesn't usually go to bed late.

CAREFUL

✗The hotel doesn't has a spa.
✓The hotel doesn't have a spa.
✗Does Suzan works in your office?
✓Does Suzan work in your office?

Present Simple is used

all cases and time expressions
🏢
permanent situations or states

She works in an office.

She works as a nurse.

She owns a large shop.

🔁
repeated / habitual actions (especially with frequency adverbs: often, usually, etc.)

He often buys her flowers.

I usually get up at 7:30 am.

🌍
permanent truths or laws of nature

The Sun sets in the west.

Money doesn't buy happiness.

Water freezes at 0°C.

🚆
timetables / programmes (future meaning)

The lesson starts at 10 o'clock.

The match finishes at 7:45 pm.

The plane leaves at 6:05 am.

🎬
reviews / sports commentaries / dramatic narratives

Angelina Jolie acts brilliantly in this film.

Past Simple Spelling

-ed endings and irregular verbs

SPELLING

like, hate → liked, hated-e: add -d only
prefer, stop → preferred, stoppedone stressed vowel + consonant: double the consonant and add -ed
carry, study → carried, studiedconsonant + y: change y to i and add -ied
play, enjoy → played, enjoyedvowel + y: simply add -ed

IRREGULAR VERBS

Irregular verbs have a special past form. It cannot be built by a rule and has to be learned.

I go → I went
I drink → I drank
I am → I was

Past Simple is used

all cases and time expressions
📆
for actions which happened at a stated time in the past

He sold his car two weeks ago.

She called an hour ago.

When? Two weeks ago.
🏠
to express a past state or habit

When he was young, he lived in a small flat.

He used to go / went to school on foot.

🔗
for past actions which happened one after the other

She put on her coat, took her bag and left the house.

She sealed the letter, put a stamp on it and posted it.

🔒
for an action which happened in the past and cannot be repeated

I once spoke to Princess Diana.

Shakespeare wrote a lot of plays.

The action was completed in the past.
